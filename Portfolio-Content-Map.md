## 10. Page / Section Relationships

Visitors move through the site in this order:

- **Home** is the entry point. The hero's **View Projects** goes to **Projects**; **Contact Me** goes to **Contact**.
- **Home** previews link onward: the projects preview opens individual case studies or Projects, and the skills preview opens **Skills**.
- **Projects** lists the three project cards. Each card's **View Case Study** opens that project's case study.
- **Case study** pages offer **View GitHub** and **View Demo** (if they exist), then **View All Projects** to return and **Contact Me** to finish. A visitor can go straight from any case study to Contact.
- **Skills** ties back to the work: skills confirmed by a project can link to that project's case study.
- **About** supports the projects with background and education, and ends with **View Projects** and **Contact Me**.
- **Contact** is the final destination. It is reachable from the main navigation on every page.

Summary path for the main visitor: Home, then Projects, then a case study, then Contact.
